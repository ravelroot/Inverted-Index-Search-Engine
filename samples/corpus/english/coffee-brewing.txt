Coffee brewing basics

Good coffee depends on four variables: the ratio of coffee to water, the grind size, the water temperature and the contact time.

A common starting ratio is one gram of coffee for every sixteen grams of water. Use water just off the boil, between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius.

Grind size must match the brewing method. Espresso needs a fine grind and about 25 seconds of extraction under pressure. A French press uses a coarse grind and four minutes of immersion. Pour-over methods sit in between, with a medium grind and a total brew time of about three minutes.

If the coffee tastes sour, it is under-extracted: grind finer or brew longer. If it tastes bitter, it is over-extracted: grind coarser or shorten the brew.
